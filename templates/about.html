About & Terms

Task To Reward is a transparent platform designed to help users earn by completing simple, verified tasks. We believe in building a long-term, trust-based community.

Manual Verification Policy

All task submissions are manually reviewed by our admin team to ensure quality and authenticity. This process can take between 24 to 72 hours.

Fake Screenshot Rejection

Submitting fake, manipulated, or incorrect screenshots will result in immediate rejection of the task. Repeated attempts may lead to permanent account blocking.

One Account Rule

Users are strictly allowed only one account per person. Creating multiple accounts to exploit the system will result in all accounts being blocked and earnings forfeited.

Final Decision

The admin's decision on task approval and payment status is final and binding.

Earnings & Payments

There are no guaranteed earnings. Payments are processed within 24–72 hours after a successful withdrawal request. The minimum withdrawal amount is subject to change by the admin.
